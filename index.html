Backups fail quietly. Disasters do not wait.

Most businesses find out their backup was not working on the day they need it. We design around the three ways data is usually lost.

Backups that quietly stop

A backup job can fail for weeks without anyone noticing. We watch every backup and are alerted as soon as one goes missing.

One building, one disaster

Fire, flood, theft or a power surge can take your data and its only backup together. A copy kept outside your premises lets your business recover.

Ransomware goes for backups first

Attackers delete or encrypt backups before demanding payment. Locked snapshots and a separate offsite copy are designed so you can still recover.

Three layers, one service

Each layer covers a different way things go wrong. Together they are watched by one monitoring system.

HybriX Vault

A backup server in your office that copies your data automatically. Snapshots are locked, so ransomware or a stolen password cannot delete them. Getting back a deleted folder is quick, because the copy is on your own network.

HybriX CloudSync

An encrypted copy of your data kept outside your premises and updated automatically. If your office is lost, your data is not.

HybriX Shield

A managed firewall and a separate network for your servers and storage, with secured remote access, so one infected laptop or the guest Wi-Fi cannot reach your data.

Questions we are often asked

Do I need to buy new hardware?

Not always. The audit shows what you have and what is worth keeping. We can supply hardware for you to buy, or rent it to you on a term.

Can you stop ransomware?

No one can promise that. What we do is make sure you can recover: locked snapshots that attackers cannot delete, a separate offsite copy, and restore tests that prove it works.

Where is the offsite copy kept?

In encrypted form with a cloud storage provider. We confirm the provider and the location in your proposal before you decide.

What happens when something fails?

Our monitoring alerts us when a backup, a drive or a connection fails. We contact you and fix it. Response times are agreed in your service agreement.

How long would a recovery take?

It depends on how much data you have and how fast your internet is. The audit gives you an estimate for each type of failure, and the restore test shows it in practice.

Can you see my files?

No. Our dashboard shows status and sizes only (whether backups ran, how full the storage is), never your file names or contents.

What about computers, CCTV and networking?

We still supply, install and support them. See the services section below.
